About Our School

A Strong Foundation for Lifelong Success

Located in the serene environment of Donyo Sabuk, Maendeleo Primary & Junior Secondary School is dedicated to providing quality, learner-centered education under the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC).

We believe every learner possesses unique talents. Through dedicated mentorship, active student leadership, and strong guidance and counseling, we nurture disciplined, confident, and innovative citizens.

🎯 Our Mission

To foster academic excellence, practical skills, and strong moral values in a supportive learning environment.

🔭 Our Vision

To be a premier educational institution producing holistic, self-reliant, and responsible leaders.

Academic Offerings

Structured for Every Stage of Growth

Our curriculum is carefully designed to guide learners smoothly from early foundational years through Junior Secondary School.
